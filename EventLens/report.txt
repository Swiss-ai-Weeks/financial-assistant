**Tesla (TSLA) 3-Year Market Anomaly Analysis**
*Period: 2023-09-22 to 2026-09-21*

### 1. Key Findings

- **Total anomalies detected:** 41
- **Positive anomalies:** 24
- **Negative anomalies:** 17

**Methodology:** Anomalies were identified using a rolling window of 20 trading days. An event was flagged when the absolute volume z-score exceeded 2 and the absolute daily return percentage exceeded 2. The first 20 trading days of the period were excluded from volume z-score calculations due to insufficient rolling history.

### 3. Key Observations

- The analysis identified 41 total anomalies over the 3-year period, with positive anomalies (24) outnumbering negative ones (17).
- Volume spikes and price movements exceeding 2 standard deviations from rolling means were the primary detection criteria.
- The dataset shows a distribution of 24 instances where price and volume moved favorably and 17 instances where the movement was negative.
- No causal inferences regarding market sentiment, trading pressure, or price direction precedence should be drawn from these daily co-occurrences alone.
- Statistical observations are limited to the co-occurrence of volume and return thresholds as defined by the rolling window methodology.
