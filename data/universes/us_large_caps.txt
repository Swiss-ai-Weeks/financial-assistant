# ~140 liquid US names, grouped so that economically related

#
# Groups are built around classic same-driver pairs: oil
# majors, refiners, pipelines, railroads, hotels, cruise
# lines, homebuilders, rating agencies, exchanges, chip
# equipment. Being listed here only makes a pair a CANDIDATE.
# Whether it is a relationship is decided by the data: on the
# 2025-09 to 2026-08 window only 8 of 52 classic pairs passed
# (JPM/BAC, CRM/ADBE, AVGO/NVDA, HLT/MAR, KLAC/LRCX, UNP/CSX,
# CVX/COP, GDX/GLD) and KO/PEP, HD/LOW, V/MA did not.
#
# A header reads "# Sector · group". The sector is an economic
# prior: two names in the same sector are tested at a looser
# correlation than two names from different ones.
